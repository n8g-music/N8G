# N8G Brand Bible

> *The definitive source of truth. Every decision flows from here. Nothing is arbitrary. Everything has meaning.*

## 1. Mission & Vision

### Why N8G Exists

N8G exists because music has been reduced to content — disposable, algorithmic, faceless in the wrong way. We reclaim the face by removing it. We restore presence through absence. We are not a band. We are not a brand. We are an environment. Every release is a world you enter, not a track you consume.

Johannesburg is our ground. The city's contradictions — its gold and its grit, its skyline and its soil — are the tension our sound lives inside. We are African contemporary. That is not a genre tag. It is a commitment to make work that could only come from this place, at this moment, made by these hands.

### The Anonymous Collective

We are three. We are never named. We are never seen without the mask. The anonymity is not a gimmick — it is the mechanism that removes ego from the equation. When you cannot tell who is who, you stop listening to *personalities* and start listening to the *work*. The audience becomes the fourth member because there is no frontperson to worship. There is only the sound, the space, the moment.

### Core Philosophy

> **Music is not entertainment. It is an environment.**

Every N8G release constructs a world. The album art, the symbols, the stage design, the silence between tracks — all of it is architecture. The listener does not consume N8G. The listener enters N8G.

Our work operates on five principles:

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Energy** | The raw current that moves through everything we make. Not aggression — vitality. |
| **Aether** | The space between. The negative. The silence that makes the sound matter. |
| **Limitlessness** | We refuse the boundaries of genre, format, expectation. The mask liberates us from biography. |
| **Presence** | Full attention. Every note is placed, never scattered. Every visual is composed, never decorated. |
| **Resonance** | The vibration that continues after the sound stops. The thing that stays with you. |

## 3. Design Language

### Colour System

Our palette is drawn from the materials of Johannesburg: its geology, its architecture, its light.

| Colour | Hex | Role | Meaning |
|--------|-----|------|---------|
| **Copper** | `#B87333` | Primary accent | Warmth, conductivity, earth, value — the metal that built this city |
| **Deep Copper** | `#8B4513` | Secondary accent | Aged copper, depth, patina, time |
| **Burnished Copper** | `#D4956A` | Highlight | Polished copper catching light — rare, intentional |
| **Charcoal** | `#1A1A1A` | Primary ground | The void that makes everything visible. Never pure black. |
| **Deep Charcoal** | `#0D0D0D` | Deep ground | For immersive darkness — album backgrounds, stage floors |
| **Clay** | `#A0522D` | Warm earth | The soil. Grounding. The red earth of the Highveld. |
| **Stone** | `#8B8581` | Neutral mid | Built surfaces. Concrete, rock, the city's skeleton. |
| **Warm Stone** | `#C4B7A6` | Light neutral | Sun-bleached surfaces. Aged walls. |
| **Sand** | `#D4C5A9` | Light accent | The colour of light on dust. Rare — never dominant. |
| **Natural Linen** | `#F5F0E8` | Paper/ground light | For text backgrounds. Raw, unbleached, warm. |
| **Wood** | `#5C4033` | Organic dark | Craft. Handwork. The warmth of natural material. |
| **Concrete Grey** | `#9B9994` | Architectural | Urban texture. Modernist restraint. |
| **Glass** | `#E8E4DF` | Translucent light | Transparency. Air. Used sparingly in overlays. |
| **Brushed Metal** | `#B0AEAA` | Industrial | Cold precision. A counterpoint to copper's warmth. |

**Usage Rules:**
- Copper (`#B87333`) is the defining accent. It appears in every N8G visual — but it is never loud. It is a thread, not a splash.
- Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) is the default ground. Pure black (`#000000`) is never used.
- Natural Linen (`#F5F0E8`) is the only permitted light background. Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) is never used.
- No colour exists for decoration alone. Every colour present has material meaning.
- The palette is constrained — never more than 4 colours in a composition plus the ground.

### Typography

The typographic system reflects the N8G duality: the architectural and the organic, the future and the hand.

**Primary Sans-Serif (Headings, Navigation, Functional Text):**
- *Spec:* A clean architectural sans-serif — geometric but humanist. No decorative terminals. Tight tracking.
- *Reference:* Something in the family of Inter, Suisse Int'l, or Founders Grotesk.
- *Usage:* All headings (H1–H4), navigation elements, metadata, track listings, symbol labels.

**Primary Serif (Body, Editorial, Manifesto Text):**
- *Spec:* A warm, humanist serif. Open counters, generous x-height, visible weight in the hairlines.
- *Reference:* Something in the family of Tiempos Text, Portrait, or Sharp Grotesk's serif cousin.
- *Usage:* Body copy above 14px, manifesto quotes, creative statements, album liner notes.

**Hierarchy & Rules:**

| Level | Family | Size | Weight | Tracking | Case |
|-------|--------|------|--------|----------|------|
| H1 | Sans-Serif | 48–72px | Light (300) | +5% | Upper |
| H2 | Sans-Serif | 28–36px | Regular (400) | +3% | Upper |
| H3 | Sans-Serif | 18–24px | Medium (500) | +2% | Title Case |
| H4 | Sans-Serif | 14–16px | Medium (500) | +5% | Upper |
| Body | Serif | 16–20px | Regular (400) | 0% | Sentence case |
| Body Small | Serif | 13–15px | Regular (400) | 0% | Sentence case |
| Label | Sans-Serif | 10–12px | Medium (500) | +10% | Upper |
| Symbol Text | Sans-Serif | Variable | Light (300) | +15% | Upper |

**Typography Rules:**
- Mixed-case headlines are forbidden. All headings are either full uppercase or title case using the rules above.
- Line height is always 1.2–1.4 for headings, 1.6–1.8 for body.
- No italic body text. Italic sans-serif and serif are reserved exclusively for album titles, foreign words, and creative emphasis — never for emphasis within body copy (use weight instead).
- No underlining. Ever. Links are indicated through copper colour shift or subtle rules.
- Kerning must be optically adjusted for all display type (H1, H2).

### Composition

**The Grid:**
- All layouts sit on a 12-column grid with generous gutters.
- The primary layout is asymmetrical: weight favours the left third or the bottom half.
- Centred composition is reserved for the manifesto, the mask, and the symbol — moments of ritual significance.
- When in doubt, push content to the edges and let the centre breathe.

**Negative Space:**
- Negative space is never "empty." It is aether — charged, intentional.
- Minimum breathing room around any key element: 2x the element's own height/width.
- Margins on editorial layouts: 8–12% of the format width.
- When space feels "too much," it is approaching correct.

**Asymmetry vs. Balance:**
- The default mode is dynamic asymmetry — a heavy element offset by open space, never mirrored.
- Symmetry is used only for focal moments: the mask, the N8G mark, the closing frame.
- One strong diagonal per composition is the invisible rule — the eye should move.

### Lighting

- **Key light:** Always warm. Copper-toned. 2800–3200K.
- **Fill:** Minimal. Let shadows hold weight. 2:1 to 4:1 lighting ratios.
- **Rim light:** Cool contrast — a hint of 5000K at the edge of the mask, the shoulder, the hand.
- **Reference:** The lighting language of Georges de La Tour — single source, dramatic falloff, the warmth concentrated where meaning lives.
- **Never:** Flat lighting. Ring lights. Overhead fluorescence. The light must feel sourced — candle, fire, dawn, tungsten.

### Patterns

N8G patterns are drawn from African geometric traditions but reduced to their structural essence. They are never decorative — they carry symbolic weight.

**Pattern Types:**
1. **The Line:** Parallel lines in groups of 3. Represents the collective. Used in borders, dividers, stage architecture.
2. **The Triangle Field:** Interlocking triangles, open. Represents energy and movement. Used in textile interpretations, beadwork references, background textures.
3. **The Concentric Arc:** Stepped arcs radiating from a single point. Represents resonance. Used in symbol compositions, album art motifs.
4. **The Grid Variation:** A disrupted grid — some cells filled, some open. Represents the city. Used sparingly in digital contexts, never in print.

**Pattern Usage Rules:**
- Patterns always appear at low opacity — 5–15% — as texture, never as dominant graphic elements.
- Patterns are always monochrome: charcoal on ground, or copper on charcoal.
- No pattern is used across more than two surfaces in a single project.
- Patterns evolve per release but never contradict the four types above.

### Photography & Imagery

**What We Photograph:**
- Hands: in motion, at rest, holding instruments, touching surfaces
- Texture: copper, clay, stone, fabric, skin (hands only), wood grain
- Space: empty rooms, corridors, stages before performance, Johannesburg architecture
- Light: the way it falls on surfaces, through dust, across a mask
- Movement: blurred hands, fabric in motion, the trail of a gesture

**What We Never Photograph:**
- Faces. Under any circumstance. Not in reflection, not in silhouette, not obscured by shadow. The mask is the face.
- Eyes looking directly at camera — even masked, the camera does not confront.
- Crowds, audiences, people in groups — N8G exists in relation to the individual, not the mass.
- Branded objects, logos, commercial spaces.
- Anything that dates the image: current phones, visible technology, fashion trends.

**Photographic Treatment:**
- Colour grading: warm shift. Copper in the highlights, deep charcoal in the shadows. Desaturated by 10–20% — never vivid.
- Grain: subtle film grain at 3–5%. Avoids digital sterility.
- Depth of field: shallow. The point of focus is specific, the rest falls to atmosphere.
- Crop: editorial. No standard ratios enforced. Square (1:1) for icons/marks; 3:4 or 9:16 for editorial; 16:9 for stage/landscape.

## 4. Visual Rules

### The Copper Mask

The mask is the centre of N8G visual identity. It is never absent. It is never removed on camera, on stage, in any public-facing image.

**Design Principles:**
- Material: Copper — hammered, not machined. Visible tool marks are intentional and valued.
- Coverage: Full face from hairline to jaw, ear to ear. No partial masks. No eyeholes that reveal skin.
- Expression: Neutral, still, present. The mask does not perform emotion — the body and sound do.
- Patina: Masks evolve. Copper oxidises. That evolution is documented, not corrected.
- Construction: The mask is assembled from shaped plates, joined with visible copper rivets — never welded invisible. The craft must be legible.

### Beadwork

Always present. Always evolving. Never removed from the visual language.

**Rules:**
- Material: Glass seed beads — small, uniform. South African beadwork traditions are the reference, not the costume.
- Placement: Collar, cuffs, mask border, instrument straps. Beadwork frames; it does not cover.
- Pattern: Geometric — triangles, chevrons, stepped lines. Derived from the Symbol Bible's core forms.
- Colour: Copper tones, clay, charcoal, with one accent colour per member (see Character Bible).
- Evolution: Beadwork patterns change per release. The material and placement rules do not.

### No Faces, No Names, No Individual Identity

This is absolute. No exceptions. No "creative interpretations."

- No member is photographed without the mask.
- No member's name appears in any N8G material.
- No biographical details — age, background, history — are shared publicly.
- No individual social media accounts. No personal branding.
- In interviews (rare, selected), voices may be distorted or text-only. The mask remains.
- The collective speaks as one. "We," never "I."

### Stage Architecture

The stage is not a platform. It is an installation.

**Principles:**
- Three positions: Left, Centre, Right. Fixed. The Centre is the anchor; Left and Right are the dynamic poles.
- Lighting: Three pools of warm light. No follow spots. No colour washes. The light is architectural, not theatrical.
- Floor: Matte charcoal. No reflective surfaces. The floor absorbs light; it does not bounce it.
- Backdrop: A single textured surface — raw concrete, hung fabric, or a projection scrim. Never LED walls. Never brand signage.
- Instruments visible: Gear is part of the composition, not hidden. Cables are dressed cleanly but not concealed.
- The space between members: Equal. The triangle is the geometry of the stage.
- Audience: In darkness. Not lit. Not filmed. They are present but invisible — the fourth member, not the spectacle.

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Core Voice

N8G writes like N8G sounds — warm, deliberate, spacious. The voice is editorial but never academic. Mysterious but never coy. Grounded in Johannesburg but legible to anyone who listens.

**The voice is:**
- **Intentional** — Every word earns its place. We do not pad, we do not fill.
- **Warm** — The register is low, the tone is inviting. We are not cold minimalists.
- **Spacious** — Sentences breathe. Paragraphs are short. Silence on the page is as important as silence in the music.
- **Material** — We write about things you can touch: copper, stone, clay, light, air. Abstract concepts are grounded in physical reference.
- **African** — We use the vocabulary of this place. We do not translate Johannesburg for a global audience; we let the global audience come to Johannesburg.
- **Collective** — "We," always. Even when one person writes, the voice is plural.

**The voice is never:**
- **Pretentious** — Complexity is not a performance. We do not use long words where short ones work.
- **Marketing speak** — No exclamation marks. No "we're excited to announce." No urgency language.
- **Self-deprecating** — We do not downplay the work. Confidence is quiet, not loud — but it is present.
- **Explanatory** — We do not tell you what the music means. We tell you what it is made of and where it comes from. The meaning is yours.
- **Trend-chasing** — No contemporary slang. No internet vernacular. The voice is timeless, not dated.

### Writing About the Music

Talk about the environment the music creates, not the genre it fits.
Talk about the materials: the textures, the spaces, the light.
Talk about the process: handmade, slow, deliberate.
Never describe tempo, key, or technical production details.
Never compare to other artists.
Never use the word "vibe." Use "resonance," "atmosphere," "environment," or be specific about the feeling.

### Writing About the Collective

Refer to "N8G" or "the collective." Never "the band," "the group," "the trio."
Refer to members as "the three" or descriptively ("the member positioned centre") — never by number, initial, or pseudonym unless established in the Character Bible.
When referencing the audience: "the listener," "the fourth member," "those who enter" — never "fans," "followers," "the community."

### Sample Phrasing

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| "Our new single drops Friday" | "A new N8G environment opens this Friday." |
| "We're so excited to share" | "The work is ready." |
| "Check out our new video" | "A visual document of the work is now available." |
| "Thanks to our fans" | "To those who enter and stay." |
| "Blending genres" | "Working outside category." |
| "Inspired by" | "Drawn from." |

## 6. Application Rules

### The N8G Mark

The primary mark is "N8G" set in the primary sans-serif, light weight, uppercase, with generous tracking. The "8" is the visual anchor — the infinity form, the closed loop, the symbol of the eternal.

**Mark Variations:**
- **Primary:** `N8G` — Horizontal lockup. Copper on charcoal.
- **Stacked:** `N` / `8` / `G` — Vertical lockup. For square formats, stage risers, and symbol compositions.
- **Symbol-Only:** The abstract N8G symbol (to be developed as the symbol system matures). Used only where the mark has already appeared in full.

**Clear Space:** Minimum 1x the height of the "8" on all sides.
**Minimum Size:** 24px tall for digital, 8mm for print.

### Media Formats & Rules

| Format | Ground | Typography | Rules |
|--------|--------|------------|-------|
| Album Cover | Deep Charcoal or image | H1 title, label metadata | Copper accent required |
| Single Art | Deep Charcoal | H2 title, label metadata | Symbol integration required |
| Social Post | Charcoal | H4 headline, no body | Mask or symbol must anchor |
| Website | Charcoal ground, Natural Linen content areas | Full hierarchy | Navigation in sans-serif upper |
| Print Editorial | Natural Linen ground | H2 headline, body serif | Copper rule at top |
| Stage Screen | Deep Charcoal | H1 or symbol only | No text below 48px equivalent |

### Red Lines (Non-Negotiable)

These rules cannot be broken for any release, any collaboration, any context:

1. The mask is always present on all three members.
2. No faces. No names. No individual identity.
3. Copper (`#B87333`) is the primary accent in every visual.
4. The voice is plural. "We," never "I."
5. Pure black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) are never used.
6. Beadwork is always present on each member.
7. The N8G mark appears on every public-facing surface.
8. Nothing is ironic. Nothing is disposable. We mean everything we make.

## 7. Governing This Document

### Authority

The Creative Director agent is the custodian of the Brand Bible. All creative output — from album art to social graphics to website design — is reviewed against this document. Approvals are granted only when output aligns with the rules herein.

### Amendment Process

1. Any team member may propose an amendment.
2. The Creative Director evaluates the proposal against the core philosophy.
3. If accepted, the amendment is integrated into this document with a dated changelog entry.
4. If an amendment contradicts a Red Line, it is rejected — Red Lines require unanimous collective agreement to change.

### Living Document

This Brand Bible is version-controlled and updated as the collective evolves. It is read by every agent before producing work. It is never "finished" — it is always current.
